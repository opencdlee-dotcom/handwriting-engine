# Roadmap Archive

**Last Updated:** 2026-04-09

## Overview

Phases are ordered by impact-to-effort ratio. The self-correction loop (Phase 1) is the single highest-ROI change — pure software, no new dependencies, directly addresses the 1.75%→1.39% CER finding from Journal of Documentation 2025. Line segmentation (Phase 2) is the next structural improvement. New local providers (Phase 3) add ensemble depth. Preprocessing and writer adaptation (Phase 4) harden edge cases. Post-processing and benchmarking (Phase 5) complete the picture.
